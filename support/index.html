How we can help

We can assist with camera access, device selection, blink reminders, application settings, and unexpected technical behaviour.

Support request details

To help us understand a report, you may include the Blink Eyes version, macOS version, Mac model, selected camera name, and a short description of what happened. Include visible error text when relevant.

Your privacy comes first. Do not send camera recordings, facial images, or other sensitive material unless you have independently decided that it is essential to your request.

Privacy and product scope

Blink Eyes processes camera input locally on your Mac. Read the Privacy Policy for details. Blink Eyes is a productivity reminder and is not intended for medical use. Blink-rate results are estimates, not health measurements.
