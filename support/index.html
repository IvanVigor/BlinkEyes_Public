Your first few minutes.

  1. Open Blink Eyes and allow Camera access when macOS asks. Select your camera in Settings.
  2. Set Reminder after and Detection sensitivity. Choose whether to show a visual cue and dim the background, then save your settings.
  3. Enable blink reminders. Use the menu-bar number to pause them, open Statistics or check the optional Preview.

Screen-break reminders are separate: turn on 20-20-20 breaks in Settings if you want them.

Quick answers.

What does the menu-bar number mean?

It is your estimated blink count over the last 60 seconds, not an average for the whole session. Open Statistics to explore a longer period.

How do I change or stop a reminder?

In Settings, adjust Reminder after, the on-screen cue and background dimming. A recognised blink clears the cue. You can pause blink reminders from the menu at any time.

The camera is not working.

Check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera. Then refresh cameras in the Blink Eyes menu and select your camera.

The count seems off.

Face the camera in good light with both eyes visible. Lower sensitivity for extra counts, or increase it gradually for missed blinks.

How do I open or close Preview?

Use Control–Option–Command–V.

I see “!” or “--” in the menu bar.

A dash means paused or temporarily unavailable, for example when no face is visible. For “!”, open the menu to read the error and check Camera permission.

Still need help?

Email info@kerny.it with your app version, macOS version and a short description of what happened.

Please leave out camera recordings and facial images. Nothing is attached or sent automatically. Support privacy.
