01 / QUICK CHECKS

Resolve the most common conditions first.

Menu bar shows “!”Check Camera permission

Open the menu for the local error, then review System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

Camera did not switchRefresh, then select by name

Allow the asynchronous camera restart to complete once.

Counts seem highImprove light, lower sensitivity

Center your face and check the downward-gaze indicator in Preview.

Counts seem lowMake both eyes visible

Improve front lighting and increase sensitivity gradually.

Open or close the recognition preview with Control–Option–Command–V. If the Mac feels unusually warm, close the preview to remove its separate rendering workload.

02 / A USEFUL SUPPORT REQUEST

A few precise details help us understand the issue.

When relevant, include the Blink Eyes version, macOS version, Mac model, selected camera name, what you expected, what happened and any visible error text. A short sequence of steps is especially useful when the behavior repeats.

Please keep sensitive material out of support messages. Do not send camera recordings, facial images or other sensitive information unless it is essential and you have independently chosen to share it.
03 / PRIVACY

Support is separate from camera processing.

Blink Eyes does not send support emails or attach camera data automatically. If you choose to email us, the content you provide is handled by the email providers involved. You can request deletion of a support conversation from the same address.

Read the Privacy Policy for full details about local processing, data retention and Apple StoreKit.
