Privacy Policy

Your camera.
Your Mac.

Camera images are processed locally, never recorded or uploaded.

Effective 26 August 2026 · Publisher: Blink Eyes · info@kerny.it

Camera processing stays local.

Camera permission is used for blink detection and the optional Preview. Frames, facial landmarks and blink analysis stay in memory and are discarded after processing. They are not recorded, transmitted, sold or shared.

Blink Eyes has no proprietary account, cloud sync, remote AI or developer server. Camera and blink processing make no network requests.

What stays on your Mac.

The app stores camera and reminder preferences, including sensitivity and fade settings. Statistics contain only five-minute totals of blinks, reminders and active duration. They are kept for 30 days and can be deleted from Statistics; individual blink timestamps are not retained.

A local error log may contain technical exception text. The app never stores camera images, screenshots, facial landmarks, eye geometry, custom reminder text or a personal profile.

Purchases are handled by Apple.

Apple StoreKit provides localized prices, annual purchases, purchase restoration and access verification. It may contact Apple and require your Apple Account. Manage Subscription opens Apple's official management page.

Product and access information stays on your device, not a developer server. Blink Eyes sends no camera or blink data to StoreKit.

You choose what to send us.

If you email info@kerny.it, your address, message and attachments are handled by the email providers involved. Blink Eyes never sends email or camera data automatically. Request deletion of a support conversation from the same email address.

No ads. No tracking.

The app does not share or sell app data or track you across apps or websites. It uses no analytics, telemetry, cookies, fingerprinting, advertising or crash-reporting services. Blink Eyes is not directed to children.

Scope and updates.

This policy covers the macOS app. Changes to its local-only data model will be reflected here and in App Store privacy details before release.

Blink Eyes is a productivity reminder, not intended for medical use. Blink estimates are not health measurements and must not be used for medical decisions.
