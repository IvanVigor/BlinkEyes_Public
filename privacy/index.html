Privacy Policy

Your privacy.
In plain words.

What the app uses, what it keeps and what you control.

Effective 30 September 2026 · Publisher: Blink Eyes · info@kerny.it

Camera access and your data.

Camera permission is used only for blink-related eye landmarks and the optional local Preview. Camera images, facial landmarks and blink analysis are processed in memory on your Mac. They are not recorded, uploaded, sold, shared or retained after processing.

Blink Eyes has no proprietary account and uses no advertising, analytics, telemetry, cloud storage, remote AI, paid APIs or tracking.

What stays on your Mac.

Blink Eyes retains no camera, biometric, behavioural, calibration, preference, diagnostic, error-log or aggregate activity data after the app closes. Settings and guided-calibration thresholds exist only in memory for the active session and are discarded when you exit.

The app has no statistics dashboard or local history database. It never stores camera images, screenshots, facial landmarks, eye geometry, custom reminder text or a personal profile.

Your choices, always within reach.

Change or revoke camera permission in macOS System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera. Without permission, camera-based blink reminders and the Preview are unavailable. You can also pause reminders in the app. Guided calibration does not create a saved facial profile.

Purchases are handled by Apple.

The app offers a three-day free introductory trial and an optional annual subscription. Apple StoreKit manages trial eligibility, expiry, billing, cancellation, purchases, restores and access verification. It may communicate with Apple and require your Apple Account.

Blink Eyes receives only transient product and access-status information needed to show the purchase flow. It does not write Apple account identifiers, transaction records, receipts, prices or trial dates to files, preferences, logs or databases, and it sends no camera or blink data to StoreKit.

You choose what to send us.

If you email info@kerny.it, your address, message and attachments are handled by the email providers involved. Blink Eyes never sends email or camera data automatically. Request deletion of a support conversation from the same email address.

No ads. No tracking.

The app does not share or sell app data or track you across apps or websites. It uses no IDFA, cookies, fingerprinting, marketing SDKs, advertising SDKs or crash-reporting services. Blink Eyes is not directed to children.

Scope and updates.

This policy covers the macOS app. Changes to how the app handles data will be reflected here and in App Store privacy details before release.

Blink Eyes is a productivity reminder, not intended for medical use. Blink estimates are not health measurements and must not be used for medical decisions.
