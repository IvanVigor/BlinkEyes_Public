Privacy Policy

Your camera data stays on your Mac.

Effective date: 26 August 2026 · Publisher: Blink Eyes · Privacy contact: info@kerny.it

This policy describes the Blink Eyes macOS application. It is written in English first; an Italian summary follows. The app is a local productivity reminder and is not intended for medical use.

1. Local-only operation

Blink Eyes does not create an account and does not use advertising, analytics, telemetry, cloud storage, remote APIs, paid APIs, OpenAI services, or third-party network services. The app does not make network requests.

2. Camera and blink analysis

The app requests macOS Camera permission only to detect blink-related eye landmarks and to show its optional local recognition preview. Camera frames, facial landmarks, and blink analysis are processed in memory on the user's Mac. They are not recorded, uploaded, transmitted, sold, shared, or retained after processing.

3. Information stored on the Mac

Blink Eyes stores only technical preferences in its local app container: selected camera name or identifier, reminder timing, sensitivity, counter configuration, partial-blink option, break timer, fade options, and tutorial state. A local error log may contain technical exception text for troubleshooting. The app never stores camera frames, screenshots, facial landmarks, or a personal profile.

You can remove local app data by resetting settings where available or by deleting the app and its macOS container.

4. Support emails

Support is optional. If you choose to email info@kerny.it, the email address, message, and any material you voluntarily include are handled through the email providers involved in sending and receiving that message. This is outside the app's local processing flow: Blink Eyes does not send support emails or attach camera data automatically.

Use support messages only for assistance or feedback. Please do not include camera frames, facial images, or other sensitive information unless it is essential and you have chosen to share it. You may request deletion of a support conversation by emailing the same address.

5. Sharing, tracking, and sales

Blink Eyes does not share app data with third parties and does not track people across apps or websites. It does not use IDFA, cookies, fingerprinting, marketing SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services.

6. Children

Blink Eyes is not directed to children. The app itself does not collect or transmit personal information.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future app version changes the local-only data model, this policy and the corresponding App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.

8. Product scope

Blink Eyes is a productivity reminder and is not intended for medical use. Blink-rate results are estimates, not health measurements. Do not use them for medical decisions.

Funzionamento locale

Blink Eyes non crea account e non usa pubblicità, analytics, telemetria, cloud, API remote, API a pagamento, servizi OpenAI o servizi di rete di terze parti. L'app non effettua richieste di rete.

Fotocamera e analisi blink

Frame, landmark degli occhi e analisi dei blink vengono elaborati solo nella memoria del Mac. Non vengono registrati, caricati, trasmessi, venduti, condivisi o conservati dopo l'elaborazione.

Dati locali

L'app conserva esclusivamente preferenze tecniche e può conservare testo tecnico in un log locale di errore. Non salva frame, screenshot, landmark facciali o profili personali.

Assistenza via email

Se scegli di contattare volontariamente info@kerny.it, l'indirizzo, il messaggio e gli eventuali allegati sono gestiti dai provider email coinvolti. Questo canale è esterno al flusso locale dell'app: Blink Eyes non invia automaticamente email né allega dati della fotocamera.

Ambito del prodotto

Blink Eyes è uno strumento di produttività e non è destinato all'uso medico. I risultati del conteggio sono stime, non misurazioni sanitarie.
