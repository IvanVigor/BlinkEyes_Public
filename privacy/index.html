Your camera data stays on your Mac.

Blink Eyes is built around a short, local processing loop. Camera frames and eye landmarks are used in memory, then discarded—not recorded, uploaded, sold or shared.

This policy describes the Blink Eyes macOS application. The app is a local productivity reminder and is not intended for medical use.
02 / LOCAL OPERATION

No proprietary account or cloud processing.

Blink Eyes does not create its own account and does not use advertising, analytics, telemetry, cloud storage, remote AI, paid APIs, OpenAI services or third-party network services for camera and blink processing. It has no developer-operated server.

03 / CAMERA & BLINK ANALYSIS

Frames exist only for the instant they are needed.

The app requests macOS Camera permission only to detect blink-related eye landmarks and show the optional local recognition preview. Camera frames, facial landmarks and blink analysis are processed in memory on the user's Mac. They are not recorded, uploaded, transmitted, sold, shared or retained after processing.

04 / INFORMATION STORED LOCALLY

Preferences and aggregates—not images or profiles.

Blink Eyes stores technical preferences in its local App Sandbox container: selected camera name or identifier, reminder settings, target range, sensitivity, partial-blink option and fade options.

It also stores aggregate five-minute statistics containing only blink count, reminder count and active processing duration. These aggregates are retained for 30 days and can be deleted from the Statistics window. Individual blink timestamps are not retained.

A local error log may contain technical exception text for troubleshooting. The app never stores camera frames, screenshots, facial landmarks, eye geometry, custom reminder text or a personal profile.

05 / APPLE COMMERCE

StoreKit is the only commerce connection.

Blink Eyes uses Apple StoreKit to obtain localized subscription information, present an annual purchase, restore purchases and verify App Store-signed access status. StoreKit may communicate with Apple and may require the user's Apple Account. Selecting Manage Subscription opens Apple's official subscription-management page.

Blink Eyes receives product and entitlement information on the device and does not transmit it to a developer-operated server. Apple StoreKit receives no camera frames, facial landmarks or blink data from Blink Eyes.

06 / SUPPORT EMAILS

You decide what to send.

Support is optional. If you email info@kerny.it, your email address, message and anything you voluntarily include are handled by the email providers involved in sending and receiving the message. This is outside the app's local processing flow: Blink Eyes does not send support emails or attach camera data automatically.

Please do not include camera frames, facial images or sensitive material unless it is essential and you have independently chosen to share it. You may request deletion of a support conversation by using the same email address.

07 / SHARING, TRACKING & SALES

No behavioral data business.

Blink Eyes does not share app data with third parties, track people across apps or websites, or use IDFA, cookies, fingerprinting, marketing SDKs, advertising SDKs or crash-reporting services.

The app is not directed to children. The app itself does not collect or transmit personal information.

08 / CHANGES & PRODUCT SCOPE

Policy changes follow product changes.

If a future version changes the local-only data model, this policy and the corresponding App Store privacy details will be updated before release.

Blink Eyes is a productivity reminder and is not intended for medical use. Blink-rate results are estimates, not health measurements. Do not use Blink Eyes for medical decisions; consult a qualified professional if you have health concerns.

09 / ITALIANO · SINTESI

La fotocamera resta sul tuo Mac.

Funzionamento locale

Blink Eyes non crea un account proprietario e non usa pubblicità, analytics, telemetria, cloud, AI remota o server dello sviluppatore per l'elaborazione di fotocamera e blink.

Fotocamera e analisi

Frame, landmark degli occhi e analisi dei blink vengono elaborati solo nella memoria del Mac. Non vengono registrati, caricati, trasmessi, venduti, condivisi o conservati dopo l'elaborazione.

Dati locali

L'app conserva preferenze tecniche e statistiche aggregate in intervalli di cinque minuti: conteggio blink, promemoria e durata attiva. Gli aggregati restano sul Mac per 30 giorni e possono essere eliminati. Non vengono conservati timestamp dei singoli blink.

Apple StoreKit

StoreKit gestisce prezzi localizzati, acquisto, ripristino e verifica dell'accesso firmato da Apple. Può comunicare con Apple, ma Blink Eyes non invia a StoreKit frame, landmark o dati dei blink.

Assistenza

Se scegli di scrivere a info@kerny.it, il messaggio è gestito dai provider email coinvolti. Blink Eyes non invia automaticamente email né allega dati della fotocamera.

Ambito

Blink Eyes è uno strumento di produttività e non è destinato all'uso medico. I risultati sono stime, non misurazioni sanitarie.
