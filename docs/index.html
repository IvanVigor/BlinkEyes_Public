Quick start

Open. Adjust.
Keep going.

Three simple steps to make Blink Eyes part of your workday.

01 / OPEN

Allow camera access.

Open Blink Eyes and allow Camera permission when asked. Your camera images stay on your Mac.

02 / ADJUST

Make it feel right.

Click the menu-bar number, open Settings and choose your reminder interval. Start with 50% sensitivity.

03 / FOCUS

Get back to your day.

Let the gentle cues remind you to blink. Enable the optional break timer whenever it suits your routine.

Everything you need, one click away.

The menu-bar number

Your estimated blinks over the last 60 seconds. A dash means paused or temporarily unavailable.

Your recent activity

Open Statistics to see local trends. History is kept for 30 days and you can clear it anytime.

Keep just the cues you want.

  • Timing: choose how long to wait before a blink reminder.
  • Appearance: adjust the on-screen cue and dimming.
  • Breaks: turn on the optional 20-20-20 timer; it is off by default.
  • Pause: switch reminders off from the menu bar.

Counting too much or too little? Face the camera in good light. Lower sensitivity for extra counts; raise it gradually for missed blinks.
