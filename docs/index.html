01 / OVERVIEW

A private productivity companion for focused Mac work.

Blink Eyes estimates bilateral blinks and offers gentle local reminders during screen work. It is designed as a quiet awareness layer: the current rolling value stays in the menu bar, while settings, statistics and recognition details remain one click away.

02 / MENU BAR

Your recent blink pace, at a glance.

The compact number shows the rolling blink estimate for the previous 60 seconds. It briefly changes color after a validated full blink. The value becomes -- when reminders are paused, a face has been absent long enough, or the engine needs attention.

From the menu you can

  • pause or activate blink reminders;
  • open Settings and local Statistics;
  • refresh or select a camera;
  • open the optional recognition preview;
  • replay the introduction, view privacy information, or quit.

Open or close the recognition preview with Control–Option–Command–V.

03 / SETTINGS

Clear controls for a setup that is uniquely yours.

  • Blink reminders: enable or pause camera-based reminders.
  • Reminder after: choose the elapsed face-present time before a gentle cue appears.
  • Detection sensitivity: start at 50%. Lower values require deeper two-eye closure; higher values accept progressively shallower closures.
  • On-screen reminder: configure the calm overlay and background dimming.
  • Camera: choose a device by its macOS name and stable identifier.
  • 20-20-20 breaks: optional and disabled by default.
Sensitivity tip. Reduce toward 20–40% if looking down is miscounted. Increase gradually only when genuine blinks are missed. Changes apply without resetting the rolling count or reopening the camera.
04 / REMINDERS

A prompt appears only when the context is right.

A blink reminder is shown only when reminders are active, a fresh face is present, the configured interval has elapsed, and no closure is in progress. A valid bilateral blink clears the cue and restarts the complete interval.

Ambiguous movement can hide the cue briefly without incrementing the count, but it does not trigger another immediate alert. Face absence pauses blink reminders, break timing and active statistics.

05 / RECOGNITION

Local landmarks, bilateral validation.

Apple Vision supplies local eye contours and pose estimates. Blink Eyes combines contour geometry, independent per-eye calibration, head-pose compensation, downward-gaze filtering and temporal close/reopen validation. Both eyes must close: the more-open normalized eye acts as the bilateral gate.

InputEye contour geometry

Not skin tone, iris color or pixel color.

ValidationClose → reopen

With duration, depth and refractory checks.

Lighting, reflections, glasses, extreme pose, motion blur and occlusion can affect any camera estimate. Use the recognition preview and sensitivity control to adapt to the current setup. Preview colors are diagnostic only.

06 / STATISTICS

Aggregate trends, stored only on your Mac.

The dashboard groups rolling activity in five-minute or larger buckets and shows blink-rate estimates and reminder totals. Only aggregate blink counts, reminder counts and active processing duration are retained locally for 30 days. Individual blink timestamps are not retained, and history can be cleared from the app.

07 / TROUBLESHOOTING

Fast checks for common conditions.

  • “!” in the menu bar: open the menu for the local error, then check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.
  • A camera switch does not apply: refresh cameras, select by name, and allow the asynchronous restart to complete once.
  • Preview is closed: use Control–Option–Command–V.
  • Counts are too high: improve front lighting, center the face, reduce sensitivity and check the gaze-down indicator.
  • Counts are too low: improve visibility of both eyes and increase sensitivity gradually.
  • Your Mac feels warm: close the recognition preview; the preview adds separate rendering work.

08 / PRIVACY

The camera path stays local.

Frames and landmarks remain in memory and are discarded. Blink Eyes has no proprietary account, advertising, analytics, cloud sync, remote AI or developer API. Apple StoreKit handles annual access through Apple's system commerce services and receives no camera or blink data.

Blink Eyes is a productivity reminder, not a medical device or health measurement. Read the full Privacy Policy for precise data and retention details.
