What Blink Eyes does

Know your rhythm.
Choose your reminders.

01 / MENU BAR
60seconds

Your blink rate, at a glance.

The menu-bar number shows your estimated blinks over the last 60 seconds. Click it to pause reminders, open Settings or view Statistics.

02 / BLINK REMINDERS
Blink.Clear the cue.

A visual cue when you need it.

When you have not blinked for your chosen interval, a gentle cue can appear. Blink to clear it. Add optional background dimming; reminders stay quiet when you step away.

03 / SCREEN BREAKS
20–20–20Optional break reminders

Remember to look away.

Enable a separate reminder to look away from the screen. Choose the interval to suit your routine; the default is 20 active minutes. Break reminders are off until you enable them.

04 / STATISTICS
Today · 7d · 30dYour activity over time

See more than a live number.

Explore blink-rate and reminder charts for today, the last 7 days or the last 30 days. Review active time and clear your saved history whenever you want.

Automatic blink calibration adapts recognition to your eyes. Use Settings to fine-tune the experience.

Reminder after
Choose 3–20 seconds between a blink and the next cue.
Detection sensitivity
Adjust from 0–100% if blinks are missed or extra ones are counted.
On-screen reminder
Show or hide the visual cue.
Background dimming
Turn it on or off; choose its strength and fade duration.
Camera & breaks
Select a camera, enable 20-20-20 reminders and set their interval.

Need to check your setup? Open the optional live Preview from the menu. Lighting, glasses and head position can affect blink estimates.
