Your recent rhythm, at a glance.

Using your Mac’s camera locally, BlinkTide analyses blinks from the last 60 seconds. It is a live session signal, not a health measurement or a record of your day.

02 / CAMERA-FREE TIMER
Blink.See it. Hear it.

A gentle cue, without the camera.

Choose a timer interval and keep the camera off. When time is up, the menu-bar eye blinks green and a short, pleasant chime plays once.

03 / SCREEN BREAKS
20–20–20Optional look-away cues

Make room for a pause.

Enable a separate cue to look away from the screen. Choose an interval that suits your routine; it is off until you turn it on.

Camera detection, one shortcut away.

Press Control–Option–Command–B to start or pause webcam detection. The optional local Preview remains available to check lighting, glasses and head position.

Privacy, without compromise

Your camera.
Never our content.

Camera access is used only for Camera detection. Timer mode keeps the camera off. Camera images are never recorded or uploaded.

  • No ads. No tracking.No BlinkTide account to create, and no developer-operated network connection.
  • No history to manage.Frames, blink analysis, settings, calibration and counters disappear when you quit. Only two local timestamps delimit the 7-day trial.
  • You control camera access.Use Timer mode with the camera off, or change permission at any time in macOS settings.
