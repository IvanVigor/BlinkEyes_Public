A calmer screen rhythm

Notice the moment.
Choose the nudge.

01 / MENU BAR
60seconds

Your recent rhythm, at a glance.

The menu-bar number estimates blinks from the last 60 seconds. It is a live session signal, not a health measurement or a record of your day.

02 / BLINK REMINDERS
Blink.Clear the cue.

A visual cue when you need it.

When you have not blinked for your chosen interval, a gentle cue can appear. Blink to clear it. Add optional background dimming; reminders stay quiet when you step away.

03 / SCREEN BREAKS
20–20–20Optional look-away cues

Make room for a pause.

Enable a separate cue to look away from the screen. Choose an interval that suits your routine; it is off until you turn it on.

04 / OPTIONAL PREVIEW
LiveCheck your setup

See the signal, not your history.

Open the optional local Preview to check your setup while Blink Eyes is running. Lighting, glasses and head position can affect the estimate.

Privacy, without compromise

Your camera.
Never our content.

Camera access supports your blink reminders. Your camera images are never recorded or uploaded.

  • No ads. No tracking.No Blink Eyes account to create, and no developer-operated network connection.
  • No history to manage.Frames, blink analysis, settings, calibration and counters stay in memory, then disappear when you quit.
  • You control camera access.Change permission at any time in macOS settings.

Private by architecture

Your camera stays
on your Mac.

Blink Eyes uses Apple camera and vision frameworks locally. It does not record, upload or retain camera images, facial landmarks or blink analysis.
