How it works

Three steps. Then back to work.

  1. Choose camera or timer.

    Open BlinkTide from the menu bar and pick a reminder mode: camera detection, or a timer from every 30 seconds to every 15 minutes. Switch whenever you like.

  2. BlinkTide keeps track.

    In camera mode it calibrates automatically and shows your recent blink rate in the menu bar. In timer mode it simply counts down to the next reminder.

    14.0EST. LAST 60 SEC

  3. Get reminded when it's time.

    When no blink is detected for the time you set, the screen dims softly and one word appears. In timer mode, a gentle chime plays and the menu bar eye blinks green.

Features

Camera detection or a simple timer.

Camera mode

Your blink rate, right in the menu bar.

BlinkTide uses your webcam and native Apple technologies to detect blinks on your device.

  • Estimates for the last 60 seconds and the current session
  • A chart of your last five minutes
  • Optional Camera Preview that tells you what to fix, such as low light
  • Choose which camera to use
  • Pause or resume at any time with ⌥⌘B

Timer mode

Periodic reminders, camera off.

Choose an interval. Each time it elapses, a gentle chime plays and the eye in the menu bar blinks green.

Settings

Set up the reminder your way.

Everything is in one window, and it follows the light or dark appearance of your Mac.

  • Reminder delay from 3 to 20 seconds without a blink
  • Show or hide the on-screen reminder
  • Background dimming with adjustable strength and fade
  • Optional 20-20-20 breaks, every 5 to 60 minutes

Your last five minutes

A small chart in the menu shows your recent blink activity. It lives in memory only.

Quiet when you step away

When your face isn't in view, BlinkTide pauses by itself. You won't come back to a screen asking you to blink.

Calibrates itself, or with you

Calibration is automatic. For a closer fit, choose Calibrate Detection and follow four guided steps.

Detection sensitivity

If blinks are missed or counted too often, move one slider, right from the menu.

Look-away breaks

Turn on optional 20-20-20 breaks: at each interval, a cue invites you to look into the distance for 20 seconds.

Opens quietly at login

With Open at login, BlinkTide waits in the menu bar. The camera stays off and no reminders run until you start them.

FAQ

Before you try it.

Still have a question? Visit Support or write to info@kerny.it.

Does BlinkTide record or upload my camera?

No. Camera frames are processed in memory on your Mac and BlinkTide doesn't use facial recognition. No camera image, blink measurement or BlinkTide content leaves your Mac.

Do I have to use the camera?

No. Timer mode keeps the camera off and reminds you with a gentle chime and a green eye cue in the menu bar, from every 30 seconds to every 15 minutes.

How accurate is the blink count?

It's an estimate, not an exact count. Lighting, eyewear and camera position can affect it. Camera Preview shows how well your eyes are detected, and you can adjust the sensitivity at any time.

What happens when the free trial ends?

You start the trial through the App Store with no charge, and nothing renews automatically. After 7 days, continuing to use BlinkTide requires a one-time €9.99 purchase. The trial is available once per Apple Account.

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay €9.99 once, and access doesn't expire or renew. Apple handles the payment and restoring your purchase.

Do I need an account?

You don't need a BlinkTide account. The free trial and the purchase use your Apple Account.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Blink detection doesn't need an internet connection. Starting the trial, buying and restoring your purchase go through Apple.

Does BlinkTide remember my settings?

Settings and calibration last for the current session and reset when you quit. BlinkTide keeps no history of your blinks. Between launches it keeps only an empty marker that you finished the tutorial, and macOS itself remembers whether BlinkTide opens at login.

Which Macs are supported?

BlinkTide requires macOS 14 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon, including every MacBook Air and MacBook Pro from the M1 models of 2020 onward. Intel-based Macs aren't supported. Camera mode needs a camera; you can choose which one to use in Settings.

Is there a Windows version?

Not yet. A Windows version is coming. For information, write to the developer at info@kerny.it.

Is BlinkTide a medical product?

No. BlinkTide is a productivity reminder. It isn't a medical device, doesn't diagnose any condition and isn't a substitute for advice from a healthcare professional.
