How it works

Pick a mode.
BlinkTide handles the rest.

  1. 1

    Choose camera or timer.

    Open BlinkTide from the menu bar and select a reminder mode: camera detection, or a timer that runs from every 30 seconds to every 15 minutes. You can switch at any time.

  2. 2

    BlinkTide starts tracking.

    In camera mode, BlinkTide calibrates automatically, and you can run a guided calibration whenever you like. Blinks are detected on your Mac, and your recent blink rate appears in the menu bar. In timer mode, BlinkTide simply keeps time until the next reminder.

  3. 3

    Get reminded when it's time.

    In camera mode, a reminder appears when BlinkTide hasn't detected a blink for the time you set. In timer mode, a gentle chime plays and the eye in the menu bar blinks green at the interval you chose. To pause reminders at any time, press ⌥⌘B.

Calibration on demand

Prefer to do it yourself? Click Calibrate Detection in the menu to start the guided procedure whenever you like.

Detection sensitivity

If blinks are missed or counted too often, adjust the sensitivity slider right from the menu.

Your last five minutes

FAQ

Before you
try it.

Still have a question? Visit Support or write to info@kerny.it.

Does BlinkTide record or upload my camera?

No. Camera frames are processed in memory on your Mac and BlinkTide doesn't use facial recognition. No camera image, blink measurement or BlinkTide content leaves your Mac.

Do I have to use the camera?

No. Timer mode keeps the camera off and reminds you with a gentle chime and a green eye cue in the menu bar, from every 30 seconds to every 15 minutes.

How accurate is the blink count?

It's an estimate, not an exact count. Lighting, eyewear and camera position can affect it. Camera Preview shows how well your eyes are detected, and you can adjust the sensitivity at any time.

What happens when the free trial ends?

You start the trial through the App Store with no charge, and nothing renews automatically. After 7 days, continuing to use BlinkTide requires a one-time €9.99 purchase. The trial is available once per Apple Account.

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay €9.99 once, and access doesn't expire or renew. Apple handles the payment and restoring your purchase.

Do I need an account?

You don't need a BlinkTide account. The free trial and the purchase use your Apple Account.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Blink detection doesn't need an internet connection. Starting the trial, buying and restoring your purchase go through Apple.

Does BlinkTide remember my settings?

Settings and calibration last for the current session and are discarded when you quit the app. BlinkTide keeps no history of your blinks.

Which Macs are supported?

BlinkTide requires macOS 14 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon, including every MacBook Air and MacBook Pro from the M1 models of 2020 onward. Intel-based Macs aren't supported. Camera mode needs a camera; you can choose which one to use in Settings.

Is there a Windows version?

Not yet. A Windows version is coming. For information, write to the developer at info@kerny.it.

Is BlinkTide a medical product?

No. BlinkTide is a productivity reminder. It isn't a medical device, doesn't diagnose any condition and isn't a substitute for advice from a healthcare professional.
