How it works

Pick a mode.
BlinkTide handles the rest.

  1. 1

    Choose camera or timer.

    Open BlinkTide from the menu bar and select a reminder mode: camera detection, or a timer that runs from every 30 seconds to every 15 minutes. You can switch at any time.

  2. 2

    BlinkTide starts tracking.

    In camera mode, BlinkTide calibrates automatically, and you can run a guided calibration whenever you like. Blinks are detected on your Mac, and your recent blink rate appears in the menu bar. In timer mode, BlinkTide simply keeps time until the next reminder.

  3. 3

    Get reminded when it's time.

    In camera mode, a reminder appears when BlinkTide doesn't detect a blink. In timer mode, you get a sound and an on-screen reminder at the interval you chose. To pause reminders at any time, press ⌥⌘B.
